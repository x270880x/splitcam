Quick answer
To become a VTuber you need three things — and two of them are free:
  1. An avatar + face tracking — rig a Live2D model in VTube Studio (2D) or a 3D model in VSeeFace. Your webcam drives the expressions.
  2. A broadcast layer — add the avatar to free SplitCam, then remove the background with AI (no green screen) and build your scene.
  3. Go live — multistream to Twitch + YouTube at once, or pick "SplitCam Camera" in Zoom / Discord to appear as your avatar in any call.

How to be a VTuber in 5 steps

From "I have an idea for a character" to live on stream in about 10 minutes — assuming you already have an avatar. The avatar app does the rigging; SplitCam does the broadcast.

1
Pick and rig your avatar varies

Start with the character. For a 2D Live2D avatar, VTube Studio is the standard — it rigs the model and tracks your face through any webcam. For a 3D model, use VSeeFace, Warudo or Animaze. Don't have a model yet? Each app ships with free starter avatars so you can test the whole pipeline before commissioning art.

This part isn't SplitCam: the avatar app owns the rig and the face/lip tracking. SplitCam picks up from its output — so any tracker you prefer works the same way in the next step.
2
Bring the avatar into SplitCam ~ 2 min

Open SplitCam and add your avatar app as a source — window capture, game capture or Spout, whichever your tracker exposes. Then drop the background: use AI background removal for a one-click cut with no green screen, or chromakey if your avatar app outputs a solid colour behind the model.

AI vs chromakey: AI removal copes with the patchy edges and semi-transparent hair that wreck a chromakey. Keep a real green screen only if you want a razor-sharp outline for a high-motion 3D model.
3
Build your scene ~ 3 min

Now make it a show. Layer game capture, a Browser Source for alerts and chat, lower-thirds and overlay images around the avatar. Save a few named scenes — Starting Soon, Main, Just Chatting, BRB — and bind each to a hotkey so you can cut between them without leaving the camera.

VTuber tip: a Browser Source pointed at your alert widget keeps follower and donation pop-ups on stream while your avatar reacts — no second app, no extra CPU.
4
Add beauty effects, masks and clean audio optional

Stack extras on top of the avatar: 3D masks, Streamfog lenses and face masking for stylised looks, plus a Timer with hotkey actions for in-stream events. Run your microphone through the VST 2/3 audio chain — de-noise, EQ, compression — so the voice behind the avatar sounds broadcast-clean.

Even-light your face: a soft key light makes tracking far more stable than a single harsh lamp. Smooth tracking = an avatar that doesn't twitch when you turn your head.
5
Go live — or join a call ~ 1 min

Two ways out. To stream: open Stream Settings, paste your Twitch and/or YouTube keys, tick both for multistreaming, and click Go Live once — the avatar broadcasts to every platform at the same time. To take a call: in Zoom, Discord, Meet or Teams pick SplitCam Camera as your webcam, and your avatar shows up instead of your face.

One setup, both outputs: the same composed scene feeds the live stream and the virtual camera — no rebuilding when you switch from streaming to a collab call.

Pro tips for a smoother VTuber setup

Small things that separate a twitchy first stream from a polished debut.

💡 Light your face, not your room

Face tracking reads contrast on your features. A soft front key light keeps the avatar's expressions stable — far more than any tracker setting.

🪄 AI background over chromakey

No green screen, no colour spill on your hair, no re-keying when the light shifts. AI removal just follows the avatar — turn it on once and forget it.

🎮 Avatar over game capture

Drop the cut-out avatar in a corner over full-screen game capture for reaction streams. One scene, hotkey to resize the avatar between "facecam" and "full".

🎙️ Run the mic through VST

A noise gate plus light compression on the VST chain means viewers hear your character, not your keyboard or room hum. Set it once, save the project.

⏪ Replay Source for clip moments

Hotkey the last 5–10 seconds back onto the screen — perfect for re-showing a clutch play or a funny avatar reaction without leaving the stream.

🔌 Wire up before you debut

Avatar app + game + encoder is a lot at once. A wired connection and a test stream to "Unlisted" first catches dropped frames before your audience does.

VTuber FAQ

How do I become a VTuber for free?

You need an avatar, face tracking and software to composite and broadcast it. A free avatar app (VTube Studio for 2D, VSeeFace for 3D) handles the rig and tracking; free SplitCam composites the avatar, removes the background with AI and sends it to Twitch, YouTube or any meeting app. No green screen, no subscription.

What software do VTubers use?

Two layers: an avatar app that rigs and tracks the model (VTube Studio, VSeeFace, Warudo, Animaze), and a broadcast app that composites the scene and streams it. SplitCam is the free broadcast layer — it adds AI background removal, scene switching, multistreaming and a virtual camera on top of whatever avatar app you use.

Do I need a green screen to be a VTuber?

No. SplitCam's AI background removal detects your avatar app's output in real time and cuts the background without chromakey. A green screen still gives the cleanest edge for fast 3D models, but it's optional — AI copes with uneven lighting that would break a chromakey.

Can I use my VTube Studio avatar in SplitCam?

Yes. Run VTube Studio as usual, then add its window as a source in SplitCam (window capture, game capture or Spout). Apply chromakey or AI background so only the avatar shows, then layer your scene and stream. The same works for VSeeFace, Warudo and Animaze.

Can I be a VTuber in Zoom or Discord?

Yes — SplitCam exposes your composed avatar scene as a virtual webcam. In Zoom, Discord, Google Meet or Teams, pick "SplitCam Camera" as your camera and your avatar shows up instead of your real face. Works in browsers too. See virtual camera details.

Do I need an expensive PC to VTube?

For a 2D Live2D avatar, a modest machine (Intel i5 / Ryzen 5, 8 GB RAM, integrated graphics) is fine. 3D models and game capture at 1080p60 want a dedicated GPU. SplitCam uses your GPU's hardware encoder (NVENC / QuickSync / AMF), so streaming itself adds little CPU load.

Can I multistream as a VTuber to Twitch and YouTube?

Yes — SplitCam has built-in multistreaming. Add your Twitch and YouTube stream keys, click Go Live once, and both platforms receive the avatar stream simultaneously, peer-to-peer, with no monthly fee. See multistreaming details.

Is SplitCam really free for VTubing?

Yes — free for personal and commercial use, no watermark, no time limit, no paid tier. AI background, beauty effects, 3D masks, scene switching, multistreaming and the virtual camera are all included from the first launch.
