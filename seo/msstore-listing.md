## 3. Description (full body)
```
SplitCam is a free virtual camera and live-streaming studio for Windows. Go live on any platform in about 5 minutes — no complicated setup, no watermark, no time limits.

WHY SPLITCAM
• Free — full multi-streaming with no paywall.
• A lightweight OBS alternative that's ready to stream out of the box.
• Works as a virtual webcam in the apps you already use: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Skype, Discord, WhatsApp, Telegram, Google Meet and more.

LIVE STREAMING
SplitCam already knows the most popular platforms — YouTube, Twitch, Facebook and others — so setting them up is a breeze. Prefer a custom destination? Just paste its server URL and stream key.

MULTI-STREAMING
Broadcast to several platforms at the same time with no extra setup. Add as many streaming destinations as you like, click Go Live, and you're online everywhere at once.

VIDEO SOURCES
Bring in almost anything: webcam, screen, games, browser, images, video files — even your phone used as a wireless camera — plus network and capture devices.

SCENE COMPOSER
Combine multiple video and audio sources on a single scene. Build layouts, switch layers, and record or stream in both horizontal and vertical (9:16) formats.

VIDEO & AUDIO EFFECTS
Enhance your stream in real time: color-correct video, blur or replace your background, add face masks and filters, overlay handwritten text or markers, and remove background noise from your audio.

AUDIO MIXER
Mix your microphone, system sound, application audio and playlists into a single clean track.

VIRTUAL CAMERA & MICROPHONE
Use your finished scene as a webcam and microphone in other apps and websites at the same time — perfect for calls, meetings and recordings.

USE YOUR PHONE AS A WEBCAM
Turn your smartphone into a high-quality wireless camera for your PC.

Download SplitCam free and start streaming today. Enjoying it? Please leave a rating — it really helps.
```

## 6. What's new (release notes — Windows v10.9.2, 2026-03-18)
```
New
• Vertical canvas for streaming and recording — horizontal and vertical canvases are fully independent, each with its own setup and audio sources.
• "Fill Canvas" command in the layer menu.
• Amateur.tv added as a stream channel.
• Blusher mask video effect.
• Animated GIF files in slideshow layers.
• Pick a restream server from a list in Restream Settings.

Improved
• More stable HTTP transport to restream servers (replacing WSS).
• Drag-and-drop of URL links without a path, text-layer translation caching, scene thumbnails, dynamic font sizing on paused scenes, GPU detection and OnlyFans sync.

Fixed
• Stream item refresh after editing.
```
