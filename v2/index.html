Multi-streaming

One stream. Every platform.

Send a single feed to Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, Kick, X, and any RTMP server simultaneously. No third-party multistream service. No latency hit. No extra bandwidth on your end.

  • Up to 8 destinations at once
  • Per-platform bitrate control
  • Built-in RTMP server
  • Custom keys, custom servers
Virtual camera

Your stream is also a webcam.

SplitCam shows up as a real camera in Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, OBS, Discord, Skype and 50+ apps. Use scenes, filters and overlays in any meeting — instantly.

  • 1-click switch to any conferencing app
  • Background blur & replace
  • Filters, overlays, lower-thirds in meetings
  • Split one webcam across multiple apps
Audio mixing

Studio-grade audio. Built-in.

Per-source volume, mute, monitoring. VST plugin support. Noise suppression and echo cancellation that actually work. Mic, system, game, and music — all on independent channels.

  • Multi-channel mixer with VU meters

  • Built-in noise gate & compressor
  • Separate audio tracks in recordings

FAQ

Quick answers.

Is SplitCam really free?

Yes — completely free for personal and commercial use. No watermark, no time limit, no upsell to a "pro" version. We make money through optional partner integrations, not your wallet.

How many platforms can I stream to at once?

Up to 8 destinations simultaneously, depending on your upload bandwidth and CPU. Most users comfortably hit 3–5 platforms on a mid-range PC.

Does it work on Mac / Linux?

SplitCam is Windows-only as of v11. macOS and Linux versions are on the roadmap. In the meantime, you can run it via Parallels or Boot Camp on Mac.

Will it slow down my games?

SplitCam uses hardware encoders (NVENC, QuickSync, AMF) by default, so encoding load is on the GPU's dedicated chip — not the cores running your game. Most users see 0–3% FPS impact.

Can I import OBS scenes?

Yes — SplitCam imports OBS scene collections in one click. All your sources, layouts, and transitions carry over.

Where's my data stored?

Your recordings and settings stay on your machine. SplitCam doesn't upload anything to the cloud unless you explicitly enable cloud backups.
