What is multistreaming?

One encode, every platform, simultaneously.

Multistreaming is the practice of broadcasting the same live stream to multiple platforms at the same time — Twitch and YouTube and Facebook, all from a single click, all with your audience watching simultaneously.

Traditionally this required either expensive cloud-based services (Restream, Castr — $10–50/month) or third-party plugins that strained your CPU. SplitCam does it natively. One encode goes out to as many destinations as your upload can handle.

If your home upload bandwidth isn't enough for every selected destination, SplitCam's built-in restream server kicks in automatically — your machine sends one feed to the cloud, the cloud fans it out to the rest. No setup, no extra cost.

What's inside

Features that make multistreaming actually work.

Built-in restream server

Don't have 20 Mbps upload? SplitCam's restream cloud takes over automatically. Send one feed, cloud handles the rest. No third-party service, no monthly fee.

Per-platform bitrate

6 Mbps to Twitch for quality. 2.5 Mbps to a backup destination. 1 Mbps to Facebook because Meta caps anyway. Tune each independently — no compromise.

Vertical + landscape canvas

Stream 9:16 to TikTok Live and 16:9 to Twitch in parallel sessions. Or run a single 9:16 stream to mobile-first platforms only. One studio, both formats.

Pre-configured platforms

Twitch, YouTube, Facebook, Kick are one-click setups. Paste your stream key, pick a server region (auto-selected for low latency), done. No URL hunting.

Hardware encoding

NVENC (Nvidia), QuickSync (Intel), AMF (AMD) auto-detected at startup. CPU stays low even on four-way streams — your game keeps its FPS.

RTMPS secure streaming

For platforms that require encrypted RTMPS (banks, enterprise webinars, sensitive content) — SplitCam supports it natively. SSL handshake automatic.

Frequently asked

Multistreaming questions.

Everything you might want to know before going live everywhere.

How many platforms can I stream to simultaneously?

Up to 8 destinations directly from your machine — or unlimited via the built-in restream server. The practical limit depends on your CPU and upload bandwidth. Most users comfortably hit 3–5 platforms on a mid-range PC with 10 Mbps upload.

Do I need a fast internet connection?

For direct multistreaming, each destination uses its own upload bandwidth — streaming at 6 Mbps to 4 platforms needs ~24 Mbps total upload. If your connection is slower, SplitCam's restream server kicks in automatically: your machine sends one feed, the cloud fans it out. No upload constraint after that.

Is the restream server really free?

Yes — included with SplitCam at no extra cost, no monthly fee, no bandwidth caps. Compare with Restream.io ($14–84/month) or Castr ($14–69/month). The restream server only activates when your local upload can't handle direct streaming.

Can I stream to Twitch and YouTube at the same time?

Yes — Twitch + YouTube simultaneously is the most common multistream combo. SplitCam ships with both pre-configured. Paste your stream keys once, then every Go Live click sends to both. Twitch's ToS allows simulcasting since June 2024, so this is fully compliant.

Can I have different bitrates per platform?

Yes — per-destination bitrate, resolution and framerate are all independent. Send 1080p60 at 6 Mbps to Twitch, 720p30 at 2.5 Mbps to Facebook, 480p30 at 800 kbps to a backup destination. Configure each in Stream Settings.

What if a platform goes down mid-stream?

If one destination drops (e.g., Twitch ingest hiccup), the others keep streaming uninterrupted. SplitCam tries to reconnect to the dropped platform automatically. Your viewers on other platforms never notice.

Can I multistream from my phone?

Yes — SplitCam mobile (iOS and Android) supports multistreaming to the same 84+ platforms. Same Stream Settings, same one-click Go Live. Vertical canvas (9:16) is the default on mobile for TikTok / Reels / Shorts.

Does SplitCam support custom RTMP servers?

Yes — any platform that accepts RTMP or RTMPS works. Paste the server URL and stream key in Custom RTMP settings. Great for private webinars, white-label streaming, or platforms not pre-configured in the SplitCam list.
