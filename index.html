Bonus: Virtual Webcam

Your stream is also a webcam.

SplitCam doubles as a virtual camera driver — meaning your entire stream output (with scenes, effects, overlays) shows up as a webcam in Zoom, Teams, Discord and 60+ other apps. Skip Windows' one-app-only camera lock.

One physical camera, every app at once.

Windows normally locks your webcam to a single application — start a Zoom call and Teams goes dark. SplitCam installs a kernel-mode virtual camera that any number of apps can read from simultaneously, with effects and scenes from your stream applied automatically.

Useful when you're streaming on Twitch and want to take a Discord call with the same camera setup, or when you need your webcam in a Zoom meeting while OBS captures the same feed.

  • Works with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Skype, Discord, Webex, Slack
  • Up to 64 simultaneous virtual outputs, zero added latency
  • Stream effects (beauty filters, backgrounds, masks) apply automatically
