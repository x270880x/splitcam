SplitCam/Alternatives/StreamYard
StreamYard alternative

The StreamYard alternative that runs on your machine, not in a browser tab.

StreamYard builds your show in the cloud, so every layout, overlay and camera feed is limited by one browser tab and one upload. SplitCam builds it on your own computer and sends the finished result straight out — free, at whatever quality your hardware can actually produce.

Short answer: what you gain and what you give up
  1. You gain quality. Your scenes are composed on your own CPU and GPU, not inside a browser tab, so resolution and frame rate are limited by your PC rather than by a web app.
  2. You gain the price. SplitCam is free with no watermark and no tier that unlocks the resolution you already paid a computer for.
  3. You give up browser guests. StreamYard lets a remote guest join by clicking a link, with nothing to install. SplitCam has no equivalent — that is its genuine advantage, not a detail.
  4. You give up working from any machine. A browser studio runs on a Chromebook or a borrowed laptop. SplitCam needs to be installed on Windows or macOS.

Why a local studio produces a better picture

This is the real difference between the two tools, and it has nothing to do with features lists. It is about where the video is assembled.

Your hardware sets the ceiling

Your camera is encoded once to reach the cloud, the cloud composes the show and encodes it again to reach the platform. Two encodes, and the second one starts from an already-compressed picture. SplitCam composes and encodes once, on your own GPU, and what leaves your machine is what the platform receives.

One upload, not two

A cloud studio has to receive your feed before it can send anything anywhere. Your camera goes up, the finished show comes back down and out. SplitCam composes locally and uploads once, so your connection carries the stream rather than the raw material for it.

Nothing breaks when the tab does

A refresh, a lost Wi-Fi moment or a browser update mid-show is a problem you can only have with a studio that lives in a tab. A local app keeps your scenes, sources and audio routing on disk.

Recording is not a separate plan

SplitCam records locally at the quality it captures, because the file is already on your machine. There is nothing to render server-side and nothing to download afterwards.

What you actually get for free

Everything below is in the free download. There is no tier above it.

84+ platforms at once

YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, Trovo, Telegram and any service that takes an RTMP key — simultaneously, sent directly from your computer to each one.

Scenes, overlays and lower-thirds

Build layouts with your camera, screen, images, text and video files, then switch between them live. Lower-thirds and titles are part of the app, not a paid add-on.

Virtual camera into 60+ apps

Your composed scene becomes a webcam that Zoom, Teams, Meet, Discord and OBS can select — so a video call can use the same production as your stream.

Audio mixer and virtual microphone

Mix microphone, system sound and media in the app, send it to the stream, and expose the same mix to other programs as a virtual microphone.

When StreamYard is the right choice

Two cases, and they are not small ones. First, remote guests: StreamYard sends a link, the guest opens it in a browser and they are in the show. SplitCam cannot do that — if your format is interviews or panels with people who will not install software, StreamYard is built for exactly that and SplitCam is not. Second, the machine you are on: a browser studio runs anywhere Chrome runs, including a Chromebook, a locked-down work laptop or a computer you borrowed for the day. SplitCam has to be installed, and it is Windows and macOS only. There is also no SplitCam support contract or vendor relationship to point a procurement department at. If any of that describes your situation, pay for the tool that solves it.

What SplitCam does not do

The same list on every comparison here, because you should be able to rule us out quickly rather than discover this after the download.

No guests joining by link

There is no browser link to send someone. You can put a guest's Zoom, Meet or Discord window into a scene with window capture, which works, but it is a workaround rather than a feature.

No Preview/Program, no multiview

You switch to a scene, you do not cue it first and cut to it. There is no wall of source previews either. If you direct from a proper switcher workflow, this will feel wrong immediately.

No per-camera recordings, no PTZ control

Recording captures the composed scene, not one file per camera for editing later. Nothing in SplitCam pans, tilts or zooms a camera for you.

NDI comes in, it does not go out

You can take NDI as a source. SplitCam is not an NDI sender for another machine to pick up.

Some sources are Windows only

Documented on Windows: the Whiteboard, Application Audio, the Replay source and the solid Color source. Scenes, layers, the virtual camera, multistreaming and recording run on both.

The phone remote is iOS today

SplitCam Remote is on the App Store; the Android version is in the works and not released. Phone-as-a-camera is separate from that and is not iOS only.

StreamYard alternative FAQ

Is SplitCam really a free StreamYard alternative, or is there a paid tier?

It is free, and there is no tier above it. The multistreaming, the scenes, the effects, the audio mixer and the recording are all in the free download, with no watermark on the output.

Can I invite a guest to my stream the way StreamYard does?

No. That is the one thing StreamYard does that SplitCam has no answer for. A guest joining by browser link with nothing to install is not a SplitCam feature, and if your show depends on it, StreamYard is the better tool.

Why would a desktop app look better than a browser studio?

Because a cloud studio encodes your camera to get it there, then encodes the finished show again on the way out — the second pass works from an already-compressed picture, and that loss does not come back. SplitCam encodes once, locally. On the same computer and connection, that usually shows up as a cleaner picture during motion.

Do I have to give up brand overlays and layouts?

No. Scenes, overlays, images, text and lower-thirds are part of SplitCam, and you switch between them live while streaming.

Does SplitCam multistream through its own servers?

No. It sends to each platform directly from your computer, which is why there is no relay charge and no extra hop in the path.

Will it run on a Chromebook?

No. SplitCam is a Windows and macOS application. A browser-based studio like StreamYard is the right choice on ChromeOS.
