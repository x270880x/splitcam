How it works

Pair in three steps

SplitCam Remote discovers your desktop on the local network — no QR codes to scan, no accounts to create, no cloud relay.

Step 01

Run SplitCam on your computer

Open SplitCam on Windows or Mac as you normally would. Enable Remote control in Settings — that exposes a local network listener.

Step 02

Install SplitCam Remote on your phone

Free, from App Store or Google Play. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the streaming PC.

Step 03

Tap to pair, then control

The app auto-discovers your desktop. Tap once to pair. Now your phone is a director's panel — switch scenes, manage audio, go live.

Pick the SplitCam you need

Start with the desktop or mobile build — add SplitCam Remote later when you want phone control. All free, all the time.
